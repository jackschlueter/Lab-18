# COST — business memo

**Decision: watch it.**

The 10-K series shows revenue rising from 226,954,000,000 at the 2022-08-28 fiscal year end to 303,154,000,000 at 2026-08-30. Net income rose from 5,844,000,000 to 9,226,000,000, while this exercise's owner earnings measure rose from 3,501,000,000 to 9,382,000,000. Each of the three code checks passes: ROE clears the threshold every year, owner earnings stay positive, and owner earnings are higher in the last year than the first.

My judgment is that this is a business worth studying closely. Persistent cash generation and returns on equity are encouraging, but these figures alone do not prove a durable competitive advantage or explain what management must spend to maintain it. This exercise subtracts all reported capital spending from operating cash flow; Buffett's fuller owner earnings concept asks for maintenance spending, which these selected facts do not isolate. I would want evidence about customer loyalty, competitive pressure, and capital allocation before deciding to own the business. This is a business judgment, with no price conclusion.

**My decision:** I trust this Buffett-style “watch it” verdict as a screening judgment because the SEC figures reproduce, while the evidence here leaves business durability and maintenance spending unresolved.
